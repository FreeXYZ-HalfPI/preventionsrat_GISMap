# Hannover districts, Stadtbezirksrat Bothfeld-Vahrenheide, and how our solution compares

Prepared for the Präventionsatlas Buchholz-Kleefeld (developer: Mohammad B. Saryazdi). Research date: 2 November 2026.
Companion to `research-switzerland-denmark.md`.

**Reading note:** facts come from search results and fetched city pages (linked at the end). Where a page could not be read or a number could not be found, it says so. The Stadtbezirksrat pages were last updated before the Kommunalwahl of 13 September 2026, so people and seat numbers may have changed.

## 1. How Hannover is organised for resident voice and prevention

| Level | What exists | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| City | *Kommunaler Präventionsrat* (KPR): members are authorities, police, public prosecutor, council factions, associations and *"Präventive Räte auf Ebene der Stadtbezirke"*. Sets a yearly theme, can form working groups (alcohol prevention, milieu/prostitution/human trafficking, democracy dialogue point), can give recommendations to the council. Office sits in the department for finance, order and fire service. | KPR rules of procedure and member list (20.05.2025) |
| 13 Stadtbezirke | Each has a *Stadtbezirksrat* (elected), an *Integrationsbeirat* and a *Stadtbezirksmanagement* (mediator between administration, politics and local actors). Sessions are public with a *Einwohnerfragestunde* on every agenda. Councils decide matters that concern only the district and are heard on larger ones. | hannover.de district pages, Ricklingen and Bothfeld-Vahrenheide |
| Neighbourhoods | *Quartiersmanagement* in the social area (funding guideline exists; applications require prior consultation with unit 50.5 Bürgerschaftliches Engagement und Soziale Stadtteilentwicklung). "Soziale Stadt" areas between 1999 and 2019. | hannover.de Quartiersmanagement |
| Citywide tools | *Mängelhinweiskarte* (anonymous), *mobile Sicherheitskonferenz* (twice a year in changing districts), *Sicherheitsbefragung* 2023, *Kinderfreundliche Kommune* survey 2025/26 (more than 1,800 children and young people), *Kinderstadtpläne* for all 13 districts, traffic-safety concept "Gib mir Acht!". | hannover.de, Sicherheitsbericht 2025 |

**What I could confirm per district**
- Only **Buchholz-Kleefeld** has a district Präventionsrat that I could confirm by name (since 2013, three working groups).
- For the other 12 Stadtbezirke I found **no page naming a district Präventionsrat**. The KPR rules mention such bodies in the plural, so some may exist but are not visible online. Asking the KPR office is the quick way to settle it.
- Mobile Sicherheitskonferenz so far: Südstadt-Bult (8 December 2025), Ricklingen (announced), Mitte (planned for the second half of 2026). Buchholz-Kleefeld and Bothfeld-Vahrenheide have not had one in the sources I found.
- School-route safety programme: new zebra crossing at Grundschule An der Feldbuschwende (opened 19 May 2025, pushed for by the Stadtbezirksrat and the school parents' council). Further crossings: Loccumer Straße and Constantinstraße (2025), planned Drostestraße, Paderborner Straße, Waldstraße (IGS List). The article does not say which Stadtbezirk each belongs to.
- Children's voice: the 2025/26 survey says many children want safe school routes, fewer dangerous traffic situations and **better lighting**. The same two topics our map is built around.

## 2. Stadtbezirksrat Bothfeld-Vahrenheide (Stadtbezirk 3)

**Basics**
- Five Stadtteile: Vahrenheide, Sahlkamp, Bothfeld, Lahe, Isernhagen-Süd. About 50,000 residents (sources differ: 50,012 as of 31.12.2019; 50,793 without date).
- 21 elected members. Bezirksbürgermeister Dr. Wjahat Waraich and deputy Beate Herkendell (page dated 26.02.2026, before the 2026 election).
- Sessions alternate between Stadtteiltreff Sahlkamp and Kulturtreff Vahrenheide. Next dates listed: 4 November 2026, 18:00, Herschelschule (Großer Kolonnenweg 37), and 16 December 2026, Kulturtreff Vahrenheide. A *Einwohnerfragestunde* is on every agenda.
- Result of the 13 September 2026 election for this council: **not found**. The result PDFs did not open as text. Check the city's election report (Wahlbericht Kommunalwahl 2026, Band 3).

**What the council has been working on (from session documents)**
- **Lighting:** SPD motion for lighting along the whole Peter-Strasser-Allee. The administration agreed; both sides of Stadtbahn line 2 get mast lights, the first section is already commissioned (decision dated 16.03.2026). SPD motion for better lighting at the eastern Laher Kirchweg (session 5 November 2025). An AfD enquiry on lighting of footpaths and cycle paths in the district.
- **Traffic and school routes:** SPD enquiry "Weg zum Kindergarten – Schulwegsicherheit – Sicherheit für Fußgänger*innen" (5 November 2025). Joint motion for acoustic signals at traffic lights. Crossing Varrelheide (session 17 June 2026). Motions about the Laher Kamp road closure.
- **Prevention and youth:** CDU motion for street social work in Vahrenheide. SPD enquiry about staffing in the youth centres. A 24-hour opening of the Polizeistation Sahlkamp/Vahrenheide was approved by the council in 2017 (16 votes, 1 abstention); the answer from the ministry was still pending in November 2017. A later AfD list shows a similar motion rejected, so the two sources do not clearly match.
- **Order and traffic enforcement (Sicherheitsbericht 2025, Ordnungsdienst):** traffic offences 355 to 1,109, towing 16 to 59, checks 166 to 207. The report sees no sign of a worse safety situation, the picture is dominated by traffic and parking.

**Is there a Präventionsrat?** I found none for this district. Prevention topics reach the council through motions of single factions and through the Stadtbezirksmanagement. This is a real gap compared with Buchholz-Kleefeld.

## 3. Best solutions elsewhere, and how ours compares

### 3.1 What the strongest models do
| Model | Core idea | Evidence of scale or results |
|---|---|---|
| **Safetipin** (India, used in Delhi, Bogotá, Nairobi) | Structured safety audit (9 parameters incl. lighting, walk path, people around) by crowd and trained auditors, scored per place and handed to city governments. Based on the Women's Safety Audit from Canada. | Delhi: data identified 7,483 dark spots which the government committed to fix. Bogotá used it on bike paths. Mostly reported by the company and partners, **no independent evaluation found**, and no proof that fixes improved safety. |
| **Decidim Barcelona** | One public platform for proposals, supports, voting and *follow-up* (status updates). Binding participatory budget. | 2020 to 2023: EUR 30 million budget, 1,982 proposals, 76 projects selected; about 80 % execution of projects cited for the action-plan process. 2024 to 2027: 1,739 proposals. |
| **Copenhagen *Tryghedsundersøgelse*** | Annual, representative survey of perceived safety since 2009, with deep-dives (2023: lighting at night, focus on women). | Even, glare-free light helps; too much light can backfire. |
| **Høje-Taastrup *Mørkekort*** | Expert darkness analysis plus anthropological resident study. | Shows "more light" is not automatically the answer. |
| **Basel *Quartierarbeit*** | Legal duty to involve residents, paid contact points, micro-grant for resident groups. | 16 meeting points, 2 secretariats, 17 associations. |
| **Wien *Sag's Wien*** | Map-based reports with visible status. | About 93,000 reports in 2023 (from our earlier proposal). |
| **Hannover mobile Sicherheitskonferenz** | Residents send places, a 2-hour route is built, walked with police, departments and the council. | Held in Südstadt-Bult; fewer participants than the first one, but concrete needs identified. |

### 3.2 Our solution against those models (honest view)
**Where we are good or unusual**
- Combines **lighting, school routes and residents' feelings** in one open map. None of the models above combines all three.
- **Free, open data and open code**; every number has a source; privacy rules built in (no personal data, aggregated results only above 3 answers).
- Fast to replicate: the pipeline only needs an OSM relation ID per district.
- Brings **figures from three official reports** into the same map (Statistische Profile, Sozialbericht, Sicherheitsbericht).

**Where others are clearly better**
- **Staff and money:** Basel and Copenhagen have paid people and budgets. We have volunteers and no budget.
- **Representative data:** Copenhagen samples the population every year. Our perception layer is self-selected, so it is biased towards people who are motivated and online. It is a prompt for a walk, not a measurement.
- **Audit depth:** Safetipin scores nine criteria on site. Our hotspots rely on OSM `lit=no` tags, which are incomplete, and do not tell lighting quality or glare.
- **Binding follow-up:** Decidim and Basel tie proposals to budgets or decisions. Our map has statuses, but nobody has committed to act on a result.
- **Barrier to entry:** answers need a GitHub account (or a copy-and-send step). Wien, Safetipin and Decidim are built for ordinary phones and accounts.
- **Children's voice:** Hannover's own Kinderfreundliche Kommune survey asked 1,800+ children; we have no children's input and no link to the *Kinderstadtpläne*.
- **No proof of impact yet:** nobody, including Safetipin, has shown independently that the maps improve safety. We should not claim it either.

**Verdict:** not the best solution yet, but a credible low-cost prototype with a distinctive combination. The best next step is to make it more *trusted* (partner with Stadtbezirksmanagement, add children and a repeated survey) rather than more *featureful*.

## 4. Suggested next steps
1. **Offer a pilot to Bothfeld-Vahrenheide**: generic district switch (relation ID, schools, hotspots), presented to the Stadtbezirksmanagement and, after the new council has constituted, to the Stadtbezirksrat. Their lighting motions (Peter-Strasser-Allee, Laher Kirchweg) can be shown as "Themen" with status.
2. **Align with the city's tools** rather than compete: link hotspots to the Mängelhinweiskarte, offer the safety-walk kit as input for the next mobile Sicherheitskonferenz, reuse the Kinderstadtplan.
3. **Add children's and young people's voice** with schools, together with the AG Demokratie and the youth centre (Kinderfreundliche Kommune survey as precedent).
4. **Repeat a short survey once a year** with a fixed question set, after Copenhagen, and report change, not only snapshots.
5. **Ask the KPR office** which district Präventionsräte exist, and for a place on the agenda to present the map.
6. **Lower the entry barrier**: a no-login answer route via the Stadtbezirksmanagement or a small self-hosted form, with data protection review.
